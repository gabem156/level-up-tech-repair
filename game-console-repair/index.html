Supported game consoles

Current confirmed coverage includes PlayStation 3, PlayStation 4, and PlayStation 5; Xbox 360, Xbox One, Xbox Series S, and Xbox Series X; and Nintendo Switch. Select the console and describe the issue to start a request.

Available repair requests

For PlayStation and Xbox, the finder lists HDMI port, overheating, disk drive, power issue, and cleaning services. Nintendo Switch requests include battery, charging port, connectivity, overheating, screen or digitizer, and cleaning services.

Published console pricing

Published PlayStation and Xbox prices include HDMI port $80, overheating $120, disk drive $180, power issue $150, deep cleaning $60, and premium cleaning $90. Published Nintendo Switch prices include battery $85, charging port $100, connectivity $80, overheating $75, screen or digitizer $125, and cleaning $60 or $90. Final details are confirmed for the specific model and issue.
