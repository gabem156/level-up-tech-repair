Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about repair appointments, pricing, mobile service, hours, and preparing your device for Level Up Tech Repair.

How do I choose the right repair?

Browse the Repair Services page for information about supported devices and repairs. If you don’t see your model or repair, contact us to request a quote.

Is the displayed price final?

Prices marked “From” are starting prices, not final quotes. The exact device, diagnosis, and parts required determine the final price, which is confirmed before work begins.

What does the $45 appointment fee cover?

Your 50-minute Acuity appointment includes a diagnostic and consultation for $45, paid through Acuity. If you approve the repair, the fee is credited toward the repair total. If you decide not to proceed, it covers the diagnostic and consultation. Additional repair costs are discussed and approved before work begins.

Do you offer mobile repair service?

Mobile repair appointments are available in Seminole and Volusia Counties, including Sanford, Lake Mary, Longwood, Oviedo, Winter Springs, DeBary, and Deltona. A $30 travel fee applies only outside those counties. Out-of-area appointments may be available; contact us to confirm.

What are your hours?

Monday through Friday, 3 PM–8 PM. Saturday and Sunday, 11 AM–7 PM.

Should I back up my device first?

When possible, back up important data before any repair. Keep your passcode private unless access is specifically needed and you’re comfortable sharing it.
