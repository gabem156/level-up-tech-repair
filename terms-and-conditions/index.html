REPAIR SERVICE TERMS

Terms & Conditions

By scheduling an appointment with Level Up Tech Repair, you acknowledge and agree to the following terms.

1. Repair Diagnostic & Consultation Fee

  • A $45 appointment fee is required when scheduling a Repair Diagnostic & Consultation.
  • The appointment is scheduled for 50 minutes for device assessment, troubleshooting, and discussion of recommended repairs.
  • If you approve the repair, the $45 appointment fee will be credited toward the total repair cost.
  • If you decline the repair, the $45 fee is retained to cover the diagnostic and consultation service.

This fee is not a separate booking deposit. Cancellation and refund terms are set out in our Cancellation & Appointment Policy.

2. Special-Order Parts

  • Special-order parts require 100% payment of the part cost upfront.
  • The cost and payment requirements will be disclosed and approved before ordering.

3. 90-Day Limited Repair Warranty

  • Repairs are covered by a 90-day limited warranty beginning on the date the device is returned to the customer.
  • The warranty covers defects in replacement parts and workmanship directly related to the original repair.
  • Accidental damage, drops, liquid exposure, misuse, unauthorized repairs or modifications, unrelated issues, and normal wear are excluded.
  • Damage occurring after the repair may affect warranty coverage when it causes or contributes to the reported issue.

See the complete Warranty & Repair Policy for details.

4. Data Backup & Device Responsibility

Customers are responsible for backing up data before bringing in or submitting a device for service. Level Up Tech Repair does not guarantee data preservation and is not responsible for data loss resulting from diagnostics, repairs, or device failure.

Customers should disclose known issues, liquid exposure, previous repairs, and existing physical damage before service. Diagnostics and repairs may affect stored information. Customers should only provide passwords or other credentials when necessary for an authorized service procedure.

5. Pre-Existing Damage & Liability

Customers must disclose pre-existing damage or known device problems before service. Level Up Tech Repair is not responsible for unrelated pre-existing conditions, hidden damage, or issues not caused by the authorized repair.

Additional issues discovered during diagnosis will be communicated to the customer. No additional repair work or charges will be authorized without customer approval.

6. Cancellation & Appointment Policy

Customers who cancel at least 24 hours before their scheduled appointment are eligible for a full refund of the $45 appointment fee. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment and missed appointments/no-shows are not eligible for a refund; the $45 fee will be retained.

Rescheduling requests should be submitted at least 24 hours before the scheduled appointment and are subject to availability. Requests made less than 24 hours before the appointment are treated as late cancellations, and the $45 appointment fee will be retained.

Customers should arrive on time and have their device available. Customers may cancel or request to reschedule using their appointment confirmation email or by contacting Level Up Tech Repair directly. See the full Cancellation & Appointment Policy.

7. Customer Acknowledgment

By scheduling an appointment, customers acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agree to these terms and the linked policies.
