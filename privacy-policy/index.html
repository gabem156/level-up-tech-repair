YOUR INFORMATION · YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Level Up Tech Repair respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you visit our website, schedule an appointment, or use our repair services.

Level Up Tech Repair · Effective Date: November 4, 2026

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

Personal information:

  • Name, email address, and phone number.
  • Appointment and service details.
  • Billing and payment-related information.

Device and repair information:

  • Device type, manufacturer, and model.
  • Description of the device issue, condition, and reported damage.
  • Previous repair history disclosed by the customer.
  • Photos or videos submitted for repair evaluation.
  • Other information necessary to diagnose or complete a repair.

Website and technical information: When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and general website usage data. This information may be collected through analytics tools and similar technologies.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Schedule and manage repair appointments.
  • Contact customers regarding appointments, estimates, and repairs.
  • Diagnose device issues and provide repair services.
  • Prepare repair estimates and process payments.
  • Communicate service updates and customer support information.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.
  • Maintain business records and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell customers’ personal information.

Payments and Third-Party Services

We may use third-party service providers to support appointment scheduling, payment processing, website analytics, email communications, and other business operations. These providers may process information necessary to perform their services under their own privacy policies and terms.

Payment information is handled through the applicable payment processor. We do not intentionally store full payment card numbers or security codes on our website.

Device Data and Customer Responsibility

Customers are responsible for backing up personal information before submitting a device for repair. Depending on the device and repair, diagnostics or repair procedures may involve accessing device functions, resetting settings, replacing components, or other operations that could affect stored information.

Level Up Tech Repair does not guarantee the preservation of photos, contacts, files, applications, or other device data. Customers should remove or protect sensitive personal information when reasonably possible and should not provide account passwords or authentication credentials unless specifically necessary for an authorized service procedure.

Please disclose known device problems, liquid exposure, previous repairs, and existing physical damage before service.

Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share information when reasonably necessary:

  • With service providers supporting our business operations.
  • To process payments or manage appointments.
  • To complete an authorized repair.
  • To comply with applicable laws, legal processes, or valid government requests.
  • To protect our business, customers, or legal rights.

Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect customer information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no website, electronic transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain customer information only for as long as reasonably necessary for business operations, customer service, recordkeeping, warranty support, and legal obligations.

Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and analytics technologies to understand website traffic, improve performance, and evaluate how visitors use our website. You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we maintain, subject to applicable legal and business recordkeeping requirements. We may need to retain certain information for completed transactions, warranty records, legal compliance, or legitimate business purposes.

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of websites operated by other organizations.

Children’s Privacy

Our website and repair services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without appropriate parental involvement or consent.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
