Warranty & Repair Policy

We want you to know what to expect before a repair begins. This page explains the limited warranty, diagnostics, parts, and other repair terms that apply when you approve service with Level Up Tech Repair.

01 Limited 90-Day Repair Warranty

Level Up Tech Repair provides a 90-day limited warranty on qualifying repairs, beginning on the date the repaired device is returned to the customer.

This warranty covers:

  • Defects in replacement parts supplied and installed by Level Up Tech Repair.
  • Workmanship issues directly related to the original repair.
  • Labor required to correct a covered issue related to the original repair.

If a covered issue occurs within the 90-day warranty period, customers should contact us to arrange an inspection.

If the issue is determined to be covered, the affected repair will be corrected at no additional charge.

This warranty applies only to the original repair. It does not guarantee the entire device against unrelated failures or future damage.

02 Warranty Exclusions

The limited warranty does not cover:

  • Accidental damage, drops, impacts, or cracked screens occurring after the repair.
  • Liquid or moisture damage occurring after the repair.
  • Damage caused by misuse, neglect, unauthorized modifications, or attempted repairs by another party.
  • New issues unrelated to the original repair.
  • Normal wear and tear.
  • Software issues, operating system updates, or application problems unless specifically included in the original service.
  • Pre-existing damage or device conditions unrelated to the original repair.

Warranty coverage may be affected if the device is subsequently damaged or altered in a way that causes or contributes to the reported problem. Each warranty claim will be evaluated individually.

Nothing in this policy is intended to waive rights that cannot legally be waived.

03 Parts and Workmanship

Replacement parts are subject to availability and applicable supplier warranty conditions.

If a replacement part is defective within the applicable warranty period, Level Up Tech Repair will inspect the issue and determine the appropriate covered remedy.

Some repairs may require additional testing or diagnostic inspection before warranty coverage can be determined.

04 Pre-Existing Conditions

Devices may have existing damage, internal defects, or other problems unrelated to the requested repair.

We will make reasonable efforts to identify and communicate known concerns before beginning work.

If additional issues are discovered during a repair, we will notify the customer and obtain approval before performing additional chargeable work.

05 Customer Data and Device Backups

Customers are responsible for backing up their personal data before submitting a device for repair.

Although reasonable care is taken during service, Level Up Tech Repair cannot guarantee that data will remain intact during repairs, troubleshooting, software procedures, or device failures.

Customers should remove sensitive information and disable security features only when necessary for the requested service.

06 Estimates, Deposits, and Special-Order Parts

Repair estimates are based on the information available at the time of inspection. An estimate may change if additional damage or problems are discovered.

Customers will be notified of additional costs and must approve additional work before it proceeds.

For special-order parts, a deposit equal to 100% of the special-order part cost is required before the part is ordered.

The customer will be informed of the deposit amount and applicable cancellation and refund terms before payment is collected.

Deposits are not routinely required for repairs using parts already available.

07 Diagnostics and Inspection

The Acuity appointment includes a professional diagnostic and consultation for $45, paid through Acuity.

If the customer approves the repair, the $45 appointment fee is credited toward the repair total. If the customer decides not to proceed, the fee covers the diagnostic and consultation service.

Additional repair costs, including parts, will be discussed and approved before work begins.

Some devices may require additional testing to identify intermittent or complex problems. Any additional charges must be communicated and approved before the work proceeds.

08 Device Pickup and Unclaimed Devices

Once a repair is completed, Level Up Tech Repair will notify the customer that the device is ready for pickup or delivery.

We will communicate with customers to establish a reasonable pickup deadline.

If a customer cannot collect the device within the agreed timeframe, they should contact us to arrange an extension.

Any additional storage, handling, or disposition arrangements will be communicated separately. Devices will not be treated as abandoned or disposed of solely because an initial pickup deadline has passed. Applicable law will govern any unclaimed property.

09 Warranty Claims

To request warranty service, contact:

Email: info@leveluptechrepairfl.com
Website: https://www.leveluptechrepairfl.com

Customers should provide:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Original repair date or repair receipt.
  • Device make and model.
  • Description of the issue.

The device may need to be inspected before warranty coverage can be approved. Proof of the original repair may be required.

If the reported issue is determined to be covered under this policy, the qualifying repair will be corrected at no additional charge.

10 Customer Acknowledgment

By approving a repair, customers acknowledge that they have had the opportunity to review the applicable repair terms and understand the limitations of the 90-day limited repair warranty.

Level Up Tech Repair is committed to treating customers fairly, communicating clearly, and providing dependable repair services.
