Cancellation & Appointment Policy

Please review our scheduling, diagnostic fee, cancellation, refund, and rescheduling terms before making an appointment.

Appointment Scheduling

All Repair Diagnostic & Consultation appointments must be scheduled in advance through our online booking system. Customers are responsible for providing accurate contact information and device details when scheduling. Appointments are subject to availability and confirmation.

Each appointment is scheduled for 50 minutes for device assessment, troubleshooting, and discussion of recommended repairs.

$45 Diagnostic & Consultation Fee

A $45 fee applies to the Repair Diagnostic & Consultation appointment. This fee covers the time reserved for evaluating your device, discussing the reported issue, and providing a repair recommendation.

  • If you approve the recommended repair, the $45 appointment fee will be credited toward your repair total.
  • If you decide not to proceed with the repair, the $45 fee covers the diagnostic and consultation service.
  • Additional repair costs, including parts and labor, will be discussed and approved before work begins.

The $45 appointment fee is not a separate booking deposit.

Cancellations and Refunds

Customers who cancel at least 24 hours before the scheduled appointment time are eligible for a full refund of the $45 appointment fee.

Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the scheduled appointment time are not eligible for a refund. The $45 appointment fee will be retained.

To cancel, customers should use the cancellation link in their appointment confirmation email or contact Level Up Tech Repair directly.

Rescheduling

Customers should provide at least 24 hours’ notice when requesting to reschedule. Rescheduling requests are subject to availability. Requests made less than 24 hours before the appointment are treated as late cancellations, and the $45 appointment fee will be retained.

Missed Appointments and No-Shows

A missed appointment or no-show occurs when a customer does not attend their scheduled appointment and has not canceled in advance. No-shows are not eligible for a refund. The full $45 appointment fee will be retained.

Customers who miss an appointment and wish to schedule another appointment may be required to pay a new appointment fee.

Late Arrivals

Please arrive on time and have your device available for evaluation. If you anticipate being late, contact us as soon as possible.

Late arrivals may require an adjusted appointment or rescheduling depending on availability. If the appointment cannot be accommodated, it may be treated as a missed appointment.

Device Preparation

Before your appointment, customers should:

  • Back up important photos, files, contacts, and other personal information.
  • Bring any necessary accessories relevant to the reported issue.
  • Make necessary arrangements for authorized diagnostic access.
  • Inform us of previous repairs, liquid exposure, physical damage, or other known issues.

Level Up Tech Repair does not guarantee the preservation of device data and is not responsible for data loss resulting from diagnostics, repair procedures, or existing device failures. Customers should only provide passwords or credentials when necessary for an authorized service procedure.

Parts Availability and Additional Repairs

Some repairs require special-order parts or additional diagnostic work. We will communicate relevant costs, availability, and estimated timelines before proceeding. No additional repair work or charges will be authorized without customer approval.

Mobile Service and Travel

Mobile repair appointments are subject to service-area availability. Our primary service areas include Seminole and Volusia Counties, Florida.

A $30 travel fee may apply to appointments outside these counties. Customers should contact us to confirm availability and applicable travel charges before scheduling.
