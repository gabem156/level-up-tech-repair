HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before you book.

Need help choosing a repair? Send your device model and a short description of the issue.

How do I choose the right repair?＋

Use Find Your Repair to select a category, manufacturer, model, and issue. If you don’t see your exact model or repair, request a quote by email.

Is the displayed price final?＋

Prices marked “From” are starting prices, not final quotes. Final price depends on the exact device, diagnosis, and parts required. We’ll confirm repair details before proceeding. The finder shows a price only when one is configured for that exact model and repair.

What is the booking deposit policy?＋

A $25 deposit is required once per appointment. It is applied toward the final repair cost and is non-refundable if you cancel. The website does not collect the deposit. Square controls checkout, so confirm the payment screen lists one $25 deposit before paying. A separate $25 diagnostic fee may apply when diagnosis is required.

Do you offer mobile repair service?＋

Mobile repair service is available in Seminole and Volusia counties, including Sanford, Lake Mary, Longwood, Oviedo, Winter Springs, DeBary, and Deltona. A $30 travel fee applies only outside those counties. Out-of-area appointments may be available; contact us to confirm.

What are your hours?＋

Monday through Friday, 3 PM–8 PM. Saturday and Sunday, 11 AM–7 PM.

Should I back up my device first?＋

When possible, back up important data before any repair. Keep your passcode private unless access is specifically needed and you’re comfortable sharing it.
