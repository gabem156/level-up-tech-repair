Before you book.

Need help choosing a repair? Browse the repair services or send us your device model and a short description of the issue.

How do I choose the right repair?＋

Browse the Repair Services section for details about supported devices and repairs. If you don’t see your exact model or repair, contact us to request a quote.

Is the displayed price final?＋

Prices marked “From” are starting prices, not final quotes. Final price depends on the exact device, diagnosis, and parts required. We’ll confirm the final price for your specific device and repair before work begins.

What does the $45 appointment fee cover?＋

Your Acuity appointment includes a professional diagnostic and consultation for $45, paid through Acuity. If you approve the repair, the fee is credited toward your repair total. If you decide not to proceed, it covers the diagnostic and consultation service. Additional repair costs, including parts, will be discussed and approved before work begins.

Do you offer mobile repair service?＋

Mobile repair service is available in Seminole and Volusia counties, including Sanford, Lake Mary, Longwood, Oviedo, Winter Springs, DeBary, and Deltona. A $30 travel fee applies only outside those counties. Out-of-area appointments may be available; contact us to confirm.

What are your hours?＋

Monday through Friday, 3 PM–8 PM. Saturday and Sunday, 11 AM–7 PM.

Should I back up my device first?＋

When possible, back up important data before any repair. Keep your passcode private unless access is specifically needed and you’re comfortable sharing it.
